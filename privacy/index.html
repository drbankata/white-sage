Privacy

This website keeps nothing about you.

This site has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no analytics. The contact form does not send anything by itself: it puts your words into a message that you then send by email or WhatsApp, or copy. Only you decide where it goes.

Pages are served by GitHub Pages, which keeps short technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. Links to LinkedIn, Google Maps and forbaxy.com open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Questions about privacy: office@whitesagehealth.com.
