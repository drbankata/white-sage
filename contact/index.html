Questions

Before you write

What does White Sage do?

White Sage builds new hospitals, takes over and revives existing ones, and advises healthcare providers. It develops and manages multi-specialty hospitals, single-specialty facilities focused on cancer care and women's health, and medical colleges.

Can White Sage take over a hospital that is struggling?

Yes. Taking over and reviving underperforming or outdated hospitals is one of its three focus areas. It starts with a full assessment, then improves operations, infrastructure and patient care.

Which hospitals are part of the White Sage network?

Forbaxy Hospital in Vaishali Nagar, Jaipur, which operates from Amar Jain Hospital, is part of the White Sage network.

Where is White Sage based?

The corporate office is at Vipul Square, Sushant Lok Phase I, Sector 43, Gurgaon, and the registered office is in Connaught Place, New Delhi. Please send all post to the Gurgaon office.

How do I start a conversation?

Call +91 92057 73937, email office@whitesagehealth.com, or send a message through the contact page. White Sage usually replies within 24 hours.
