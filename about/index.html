White Sage develops and manages multi-specialty hospitals, single-specialty facilities focused on cancer care and women's health, and medical colleges.

Who we are

Experienced hands for the whole life of a hospital

White Sage brings long experience and a forward-looking way of working to the people who own and run healthcare institutions. Its senior team has spent decades building and running hospitals for Fortis, Medanta, CARE, Max, Manipal and others, and advising them at Deloitte and BCG.

That experience now goes into three kinds of work: building new hospitals, taking over and reviving existing ones, and advising healthcare providers.
